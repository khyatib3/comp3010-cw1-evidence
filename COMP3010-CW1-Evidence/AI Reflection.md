# AI Reflection

Throughout the investigation, AI tools were used to assist my understanding of how to approach the investigation `(qwen2.5-first-prompt-infection-vector-recognition-guidance.png)`, IOCs to identify, organising the alerts into a summarised manner `(alerts-organisation-command-guidance.png)`, `(copilot-better-command-alerts-summary.png)` and technical understanding of elements within the PCAP file `(copilot-query-unverified-checksum-meaning.png)`- these models were the regualr-access free tier of Copilot and Ollama `dolphin:mistral`, `qwen2.5:3b` & `deepseek-r1`. Multiple models had to be used due to the satisfaction of results.

`Dolpin:mistral` was the first I consulted, but a test prompt revealed its inaccuracy of information and outdated response (`dolphin-mistral-test-prompt.png`), hence was discarded. `Qwen2.5:3b` yielded results which largely gave the structured guidance I was seeking, however performance was quite slow, which led me to review CPU & Memory usage (`cpu-usage-check-for-ai-performance.png`, `host-memory-usage-check-ai-performance.png`) and reconfigure settings for the Ubuntu VM. This improved the speed at which the model responded, but was still rather slow compared to general response rates of LLMs (i.e. ChatGPT, Copilot etc.) `Qwen2.5:3b` did not provide the desired linux command for alert organisation, hence Copilot was consulted. `Deepseek-r1` was time-consuming regarding curating a response, but the output was greatly detailed and well-thought (`deepseekR1-guidance-incident-briefing-structure.png`, `deepseekr1-response-briefing-guidance.png`).

A time-consuming factor was the provision of context needed for Copilot to provide accurate responses about queries regarding the intrusion incident  - this meant first installing `tshark` to collate necessary packet information. Misinformation was a risk, resulting in cross-references with IDS alerts and PCAP. Context-provisioning was harder to give to Ollama models as I were running them in terminal, which would have tailored the responses.
